No. 05From the founderIn both seats
“

I’ve sat in both seats. Every candidate you meet has been interviewed by someone who has done the job.

The founder, 1A Recruitment

Read the founder storyClose the founder story

I started my career in recruitment as a consultant in technology staffing, then spent three years flying as cabin crew. Since then I’ve made the move from the cabin back into a corporate career myself.

On every flight I worked alongside people who had already built careers before flying: lawyers, nurses, teachers, people from tech and gaming. Flying gave them even more: calm under pressure, cultural fluency and a service instinct you can’t train in an office.

Yet almost all of us had the same worry. We knew we had the skills, but we didn’t know how to get back into a corporate role, or how to show employers that our experience in the air translates to the office.

1A Recruitment brings both sides together. I’ve sat in both seats. Every candidate you meet has been interviewed by someone who has done the job.

Q.01What does it cost to hire through 1A Recruitment?

Nothing until you hire. Our fee is 15% of your hire’s first-year base salary, paid only when they sign. There are no upfront costs and no retainers.

Q.02What if a hire doesn’t work out?

If your hire leaves within 90 days, we find a replacement at no extra cost.

Q.03How do you vet candidates?

Every candidate is interviewed by someone who has worked as cabin crew, so we know what good looks like. References are checked before you see a profile.

Q.04Do candidates have corporate experience?

Many do. A lot of crew had careers before flying, in fields like law, nursing, teaching, hospitality, retail and tech. Each profile shows what someone did before flying as well as during it.

Q.05Which roles do you cover?

Customer success and account management, executive and personal assistance, VIP and client relations, and remote operations. Roles can be remote or hybrid with companies worldwide, or in the UK for crew with a UK background.

If you have a client-facing role that isn’t listed, ask us.

Q.06I’m former cabin crew. How do I join?

Email us at hello@1arecruitment.com with a few lines about your flying, what you did before, and what you’d like to do next. We’ll reply within two working days.

Joining is always free. Your details are never shared with an employer without your permission.

You don’t need to know yet how your experience translates to an office. Helping with that is our job.
