Why ex-crew

The skills you hire for, already proven in the air.

Most of what makes someone great in a client-facing role is hard to test at interview. Cabin crew practise it on every flight.

Composure

Crew requalify every year in safety, first aid and emergency procedures. A difficult client or a missed deadline doesn’t rattle them.

Premium service

Many have served First and Business Class guests and VIPs. Discretion and attention to detail are second nature.

Made for remote teams

They’ve worked with dozens of nationalities, often in several languages, across shifting time zones. And they join a brand-new crew on almost every flight.

Careers before flying

Our crew come from a wide range of backgrounds, with a huge amount of talent from careers in law, teaching, sales, tech and more before they flew.

Read the founder storyClose the founder story

I started my career in recruitment as a consultant in technology staffing, then spent three years flying as cabin crew. Since then I’ve made the move from the cabin back into a corporate career myself.

On every flight I worked alongside people who had already built careers before flying: lawyers, teachers, people from sales and tech. Flying gave them even more: calm under pressure, cultural fluency and a service instinct you can’t train in corporate roles.

Yet almost all of us had the same worry. We knew we had the skills, but we didn’t know how to get back into a corporate role, or how to show employers that our experience in the air translates to corporate roles.

1A Recruitment brings both sides together. I’ve sat in both seats. Every candidate you meet has been interviewed by someone who has done the job.

– Amy Byrne, the founder

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does it cost to hire through 1A Recruitment?

We agree our fee with you for each role before we start, as every brief is different. There are no upfront costs and no retainers.

What if a hire doesn’t work out?

If your hire leaves within 90 days, we find a replacement at no extra cost.

How do you vet candidates?

Every candidate is interviewed by someone who has worked as cabin crew, and references are checked before you see a profile.

Do candidates have corporate experience?

Many do. A lot of crew had careers before flying, in fields like law, teaching, sales, tech and more. Each profile shows what someone did before flying as well as during it.

Which roles do you cover?

Customer success and account management, executive and personal assistance, VIP and client relations, and remote operations. All of our roles are remote, with companies worldwide.

If you have a client-facing role that isn’t listed, ask us.

I’m former cabin crew. How do I join?

Email your CV to hello@1arecruitment.com with a few lines about your experience: your flying, what you did before, and what you’d like to do next. We’ll reply within two working days.

Joining is always free. Your details are never shared with an employer without your permission.
